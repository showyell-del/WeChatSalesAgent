# AI-SPEC — Phase 3: DeepSeek Structured Analysis and Cost Ledger

> AI design contract generated by `gsd-ai-integration-phase`.

## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid — structured extraction/classification plus constrained content generation

**Description:** A local-first merchant workflow sends only selected evidence excerpts to DeepSeek, accepts one strict lead judgment, and persists an auditable score, band, evidence references, action, compliant draft, and exact usage/cost record.

**Critical Failure Modes:**

1. Evidence IDs or claims do not match supplied source evidence.
2. Draft invents prices, discounts, inventory, promises, or customer needs.
3. Malformed/partial output is repaired, guessed, or persisted as success.
4. More chat content than the minimum selected evidence is uploaded.
5. Usage or cost is estimated as actual when the provider did not report it.

## 1b. Domain Context

**Industry Vertical:** Local street-dance education and training sales; private-domain customer relationship management over one-to-one WeChat conversations
**User Population:** A street-dance store owner or senior sales operator who reviews ranked leads, source evidence, recommended next actions, personalized activation drafts, and per-call cost before any outreach
**Stakes Level:** High
**Output Consequence:** The output determines which identifiable customers receive sales attention and what a human may send to them. A wrong judgment can waste scarce follow-up capacity, expose private chat content, create an intrusive customer experience, or turn an unsupported price, offer, inventory statement, customer need, or training-outcome promise into a customer-facing representation. The score is decision support only: it is an uncalibrated `成交意向分`, not a conversion probability, and no draft is sent without human review.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

**Dimension: Follow-up priority grounded in buying behavior**  
**Good (domain expert would accept):** High-intent and activation candidates are distinguished by recent, customer-authored purchase signals that matter in a dance-studio funnel—for example asking about price, schedule, location, trial class, payment, availability, or providing contact details—with stated obstacles and recency reflected in the score and next action. Vague social chat, store-authored sales language, old resolved interest, rejection, or an incompatible schedule does not get promoted merely because it contains a keyword.  
**Bad (domain expert would flag):** A friendly but non-buying contact outranks a customer arranging a trial, outbound store copy is treated as customer intent, a negative statement raises the score, or stale evidence is treated as current urgency.  
**Stakes:** High  
**Source:** Sales-practice funnel review; lead-scoring research identifies sparse outcome labels, long decision cycles, unstructured CRM semantics, and relative lead prioritization as the real decision problem rather than pointwise probability alone.

**Dimension: Evidence-to-judgment traceability**  
**Good (domain expert would accept):** Every material score rationale, obstacle, suggested action, and personalized draft claim can be traced to one or more exact evidence IDs from the same customer packet; direction and timestamp show who said what and when. Conflicting evidence remains visible, and unsupported inference is omitted.  
**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The result cites another customer, references an ID not sent to the model, turns an inference into a fact, hides contradictory or newer messages, or gives a plausible recommendation that a reviewer cannot verify against the cited chat.  
**Stakes:** Critical  
**Source:** Microsoft Sales Research Bench treats groundedness, relevance, explainability, and schema accuracy as customer-weighted dimensions for decision-ready CRM analysis; project requirements REQ-LEAD-005 and REQ-LEAD-008 require real evidence and reject evidence mismatch.

**Dimension: Truthful, proportionate activation draft**  
**Good (domain expert would accept):** A draft exists only for an actionable candidate, responds naturally to the customer's expressed need and latest context, uses only configured store facts, proposes one appropriate next step, and is editable before sending. Prices, promotions, address, hours, availability, inventory, and service commitments are stated only when present in current business truth; training outcomes are not guaranteed.  
**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The draft fabricates or embellishes a discount, price, seat, teacher, schedule, result, urgency, or customer need; promises that training will achieve a result; pressures a rejecting customer; or exposes private profile details so precisely that the message feels surveillant.  
**Stakes:** Critical  
**Source:** PRC Advertising Law Articles 3, 4, 8, 24, and 28 require truthful, clear advertising and prohibit misleading claims and guaranteed education/training effects; field research on AI-personalized retail advice finds that perceived intrusiveness can offset perceived helpfulness and reduce purchase behavior.

**Dimension: Minimum-necessary and fair use of private-chat data**  
**Good (domain expert would accept):** Only whole, locally selected excerpts necessary to judge this customer are transmitted; unnecessary identifiers and unrelated conversation history stay local. The operator can explain why a customer received a band, correct or reject the result, and honor a customer's refusal of personalized marketing. Under-14 data is excluded unless the required guardian consent and dedicated handling rules are in place.  
**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The full chat archive is uploaded for convenience, unrelated family or sensitive details influence outreach, an inferred profile produces unexplained or discriminatory treatment, refusal is ignored, or a child's information is processed as ordinary lead data.  
**Stakes:** Critical  
**Source:** PRC Personal Information Protection Law Articles 5–8 require lawful, transparent, minimum-scope and quality-preserving processing; Articles 24, 28, and 31 govern automated marketing decisions and children's sensitive personal information; Articles 55–56 require a documented impact assessment for automated decisions and other high-impact processing.

**Dimension: Provider-cost reconciliation**  
**Good (domain expert would accept):** Each successful judgment has one immutable ledger entry whose provider-reported cache-hit input, cache-miss input, and output token counts reconcile to total usage and whose exact `Decimal` calculation uses the stored price snapshot. Estimated cost is visibly separate from actual cost, and failed or unpersisted calls never appear as completed analysis.  
**Bad (domain expert would flag):** Missing provider usage is reconstructed, cache-hit and cache-miss tokens are blended under the wrong rate, floating-point rounding prevents reconciliation, a second hidden call is omitted, or a result is shown without its atomic ledger record.  
**Stakes:** High  
**Source:** Store-owner financial-control practice, DeepSeek's token-pricing categories, and project requirement REQ-UI-005 for real input/output tokens and cumulative cost.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Intent inversion from speaker or negation errors:** The system scores the store's own offer, quoted price, or scheduling prompt as inbound demand, or interprets “not interested,” “too expensive,” or “cannot attend” as positive intent because a purchase keyword is present.
2. **Stale-context activation:** A historical question is treated as current despite a later rejection, purchase elsewhere, changed schedule, or long silence, producing a mistimed and potentially irritating follow-up.
3. **Creepy or unsafe personalization:** The draft repeats age, grade, address, family details, or other private facts that are unnecessary to the next action; personalization exposes how extensively the conversation was analyzed and damages trust.
4. **Commercial-truth drift:** The draft converts likely store facts into definite claims, invents scarcity or discounts, or promises a training outcome. Human review becomes ineffective when polished wording obscures the unsupported claim.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

- **PRC Personal Information Protection Law:** Lead scoring here fits the statutory concept of automated decision-making because it analyzes identifiable customers' behavior and interests. Before deployment, the store must establish a lawful processing basis and clear notice, limit collection and DeepSeek transmission to the minimum necessary, preserve data quality, provide transparent and fair treatment, and provide a non-personalized option or convenient refusal route for automated commercial marketing. Automated decision-making requires a prior personal-information protection impact assessment and retained record. Information about a child under 14 is sensitive personal information and requires guardian consent plus dedicated processing rules.
- **External API location must be verified, not assumed:** If the configured DeepSeek endpoint sends personal information outside mainland China, the applicable PIPL cross-border path, separate notice/consent, recipient safeguards, and impact assessment must be completed before transmission. A configurable Base URL is not evidence that an endpoint is domestic or compliant.
- **PRC Advertising Law:** A one-to-one activation message that promotes the studio can fall within commercial advertising. Store/service facts must be truthful, accurate, clear, and non-misleading. Education or training copy must not expressly or implicitly guarantee training effects. The product's deterministic prohibited-claim check and mandatory human preview are therefore release gates, not optional tone improvements.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** No AI framework — direct OpenAI-compatible HTTPS client with Pydantic 2.12.5

**Version:** Python 3.9.6 stdlib HTTP; Pydantic 2.12.5

**Rationale:** This phase is one explicit request/validation/persistence path with no RAG, agents, tools, or framework-managed state. Direct transport preserves exact provider failures and usage fields. Pydantic validates strict output without parser repair.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| OpenAI Python SDK 2.28.0 | Adds transport abstraction without a required capability; direct HTTPS is already established. |
| LangChain structured output | Adds retry/repair and abstraction behavior that conflicts with the explicit fail-closed path. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — request schema is OpenAI-compatible, provider-specific usage/pricing is explicit.

### Minimal entry-point pattern

```python
import os

from agent_core.ai_models import LeadJudgment, ProviderUsage
from agent_core.ai_prompt import build_messages
from agent_core.deepseek_client import DeepSeekClient, DeepSeekError


def analyze_one(business_truth: dict, customer_packet: dict) -> tuple:
    client = DeepSeekClient(
        api_key=os.environ["DEEPSEEK_API_KEY"],
        base_url=os.environ.get("DEEPSEEK_BASE_URL", "https://api.deepseek.com"),
        timeout=120,
    )
    response = client.complete_json(
        model=os.environ.get("DEEPSEEK_MODEL", "deepseek-v4-flash"),
        messages=build_messages(business_truth, customer_packet),
        max_tokens=1400,
    )

    choices = response.get("choices")
    if not isinstance(choices, list) or len(choices) != 1:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_RESPONSE_INVALID", "Expected exactly one choice.")
    choice = choices[0]
    if choice.get("finish_reason") != "stop":
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_INCOMPLETE", "DeepSeek output did not finish normally.")
    content = choice.get("message", {}).get("content")
    if not isinstance(content, str) or not content:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_EMPTY", "DeepSeek returned empty content.")

    # One parse and one strict validation pass. Never repair or ask the model again.
    try:
        judgment = LeadJudgment.model_validate_json(content)
        usage = ProviderUsage.model_validate(response.get("usage"))
    except ValidationError as exc:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_INVALID", str(exc)) from exc

    supplied_ids = {item["evidence_id"] for item in customer_packet["evidence"]}
    if not set(judgment.evidence_ids).issubset(supplied_ids):
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN", "Output cites unknown evidence.")
    if not set(judgment.draft_evidence_ids).issubset(supplied_ids):
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_DRAFT_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN", "Draft cites unknown evidence.")
    return judgment, usage, response
```

### Core abstractions

| Abstraction | Responsibility in this phase |
|---|---|
| `DeepSeekClient` | Build one OpenAI-compatible `POST /chat/completions` request, preserve provider HTTP errors, and return the decoded response envelope without retries. |
| `LeadJudgment` | Strictly validate score, band, evidence references, next action, and conditional draft fields; reject unknown fields and coercion. |
| `ProviderUsage` | Validate exact provider token counts, including cache-hit/miss decomposition and total invariants. |
| Prompt builder | Keep versioned system rules, business truth, and selected evidence in separate messages with canonical JSON serialization. |
| Analysis transaction | Recheck evidence membership, calculate cost with `Decimal`, and atomically persist judgment plus provenance and ledger. |

### Specific pitfalls

1. **JSON mode is not schema validation.** `response_format={"type":"json_object"}` guarantees a JSON object string, not correct fields, evidence IDs, bands, or claims. Pydantic and local evidence/business-rule checks remain mandatory.
2. **A JSON instruction and explicit `max_tokens` are required.** DeepSeek documents that omitting the JSON instruction can produce whitespace until the limit, while an undersized limit can end with `finish_reason="length"`. The fixed system prompt includes “JSON” plus an example, and the request sets `max_tokens=1400`.
3. **JSON mode may return empty content.** The official guide suggests changing the prompt and trying again; that conflicts with this phase's locked no-repair path. Treat empty content as `DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_EMPTY` and surface it without retry.
4. **Only `finish_reason="stop"` is a valid analysis result.** `length`, `content_filter`, `tool_calls`, and `insufficient_system_resource` are terminal failures; persisting any partial content would create an unauditable judgment.
5. **Usage must not be reconstructed.** Actual cost requires provider-returned `prompt_cache_hit_tokens`, `prompt_cache_miss_tokens`, and `completion_tokens`. Missing or inconsistent fields fail the call; local token estimates may be diagnostics only and must never populate the actual-cost ledger.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

### Model and request settings

Use the user-configured model, defaulting to `deepseek-v4-flash`. Send the configured value unchanged and surface a provider rejection unchanged; do not substitute another model. Use non-thinking mode for this bounded extraction/generation path:

```python
payload = {
    "model": configured_model,                 # default: deepseek-v4-flash
    "messages": build_messages(business_truth, customer_packet),
    "response_format": {"type": "json_object"},
    "thinking": {"type": "disabled"},
    "temperature": 0,
    "max_tokens": 1400,
    "stream": False,
}
```

Do not set `top_p` when setting `temperature`. Tools, function calls, web search, and provider-side file access are disabled by omission. The model receives only the versioned rules, canonical business truth, and whole locally selected evidence excerpts.

### Core request-to-ledger pattern

```python
import hashlib
import json
from decimal import Decimal

from pydantic import ValidationError

from agent_core.ai_models import LeadJudgment, ProviderUsage
from agent_core.deepseek_client import DeepSeekError


MILLION = Decimal("1000000")


def validate_and_price(
    response: dict,
    customer_packet: dict,
    hit_price: str,
    miss_price: str,
    output_price: str,
) -> tuple:
    choices = response.get("choices")
    if not isinstance(choices, list) or len(choices) != 1:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_RESPONSE_INVALID", "Expected exactly one choice.")
    choice = choices[0]
    if choice.get("finish_reason") != "stop":
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_INCOMPLETE", "Output did not finish normally.")
    content = choice.get("message", {}).get("content")
    if not isinstance(content, str) or not content:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_EMPTY", "Output content is empty.")

    try:
        judgment = LeadJudgment.model_validate_json(content)
        usage = ProviderUsage.model_validate(response.get("usage"))
    except ValidationError as exc:
        # Log the validation code/path, not customer content or the API key.
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_OUTPUT_INVALID", str(exc)) from exc

    supplied_ids = {item["evidence_id"] for item in customer_packet["evidence"]}
    cited_ids = set(judgment.evidence_ids) | set(judgment.draft_evidence_ids)
    if not cited_ids.issubset(supplied_ids):
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN", "Unknown evidence reference.")
    if judgment.customer_id != customer_packet["customer_id"]:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_CUSTOMER_MISMATCH", "Customer ID changed.")
    if judgment.recent_contact_ts != customer_packet["recent_contact_ts"]:
        raise DeepSeekError("DEEPSEEK_CONTACT_TIME_MISMATCH", "Contact time changed.")

    # Parse configured decimal strings directly. Never calculate money with float.
    cost = (
        Decimal(usage.prompt_cache_hit_tokens) * Decimal(hit_price)
        + Decimal(usage.prompt_cache_miss_tokens) * Decimal(miss_price)
        + Decimal(usage.completion_tokens) * Decimal(output_price)
    ) / MILLION
    response_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(
        json.dumps(response, ensure_ascii=False, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":")).encode("utf-8")
    ).hexdigest()
    return judgment, usage, cost, response_sha256
```

After this function succeeds, persist the following in one SQLite transaction: validated judgment, corpus/generation/customer keys, cited evidence IDs, prompt version and hash, schema version, requested and returned model names, base URL origin, provider response ID and system fingerprint, full raw usage JSON, price snapshot/version and currency, three configured per-million prices, each token component, exact unrounded `Decimal` cost serialized as text, and response hash. A failed transaction is a failed analysis; never expose an unpersisted judgment as success.

### State management

The model is stateless and every candidate is one independent call. Do not replay conversation history or let prior model output become context. SQLite is the source of truth: create a staging run, perform one call per unique `(run_id, username)`, then atomically commit the validated judgment and successful-call ledger or persist a terminal failed-call record. Never silently launch a second provider call for the same candidate in one run.

API keys stay in macOS Keychain and must never enter SQLite, prompts, logs, exceptions, or hashes. Persist the base URL origin and non-secret configuration versions so an operator can reproduce which endpoint and contract were used.

### Context-window strategy

Selection is local and deterministic before transport. Include only whole evidence records needed for the candidate, with stable `evidence_id`, direction, timestamp, and excerpt; do not upload the full conversation or the Phase 2 corpus. Enforce configured limits for evidence count, per-excerpt characters, and total canonical request bytes before making the call. If the selected packet exceeds a limit, fail locally with `AI_CONTEXT_TOO_LARGE`; do not invisibly truncate, summarize with another model, or drop evidence until it happens to fit.

Order fixed prompt/business-truth content before evidence so identical prefixes can benefit from provider context caching without changing semantics. Pricing still comes solely from the provider's cache-hit/miss usage fields.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

### 4b.1 Structured Outputs with Pydantic

DeepSeek JSON Output is integrated directly: send `response_format={"type":"json_object"}`, then pass the returned `message.content` exactly once to Pydantic 2.12.5. The use-case schema is fail-closed:

```python
from typing import List, Literal, Optional

from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field, StrictInt, StrictStr, model_validator


class LeadJudgment(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid", strict=True)

    customer_id: StrictStr = Field(min_length=1)
    intent_score: StrictInt = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    intent_band: Literal["高意向", "待激活", "长期培育", "排除"]
    recent_contact_ts: StrictInt = Field(gt=0)
    evidence_ids: List[StrictStr] = Field(min_length=1, max_length=12)
    obstacles: List[StrictStr] = Field(max_length=5)
    suggested_action: StrictStr = Field(min_length=1, max_length=400)
    draft_text: Optional[StrictStr] = Field(default=None, max_length=1000)
    draft_evidence_ids: List[StrictStr] = Field(default_factory=list, max_length=8)

    @model_validator(mode="after")
    def enforce_band_and_draft(self):
        expected = (
            "高意向" if self.intent_score >= 80 else
            "待激活" if self.intent_score >= 55 else
            "长期培育" if self.intent_score >= 30 else "排除"
        )
        if self.intent_band != expected:
            raise ValueError("intent band does not match score")
        actionable = expected in ("高意向", "待激活")
        if actionable and (not self.draft_text or not self.draft_evidence_ids):
            raise ValueError("actionable lead requires a draft and draft evidence")
        if not actionable and (self.draft_text is not None or self.draft_evidence_ids):
            raise ValueError("non-actionable lead must not contain a draft")
        return self
```

Validation has **zero automatic retries**. Log request ID, provider response ID if present, model, prompt/schema versions, HTTP status, stable error code, `finish_reason`, and Pydantic error locations/types. Do not log the API key or evidence bodies. Surface the first failure to the operator and persist it as failure metadata. Never strip Markdown fences, complete truncated JSON, coerce values, delete extra fields, modify the prompt, or re-call the model to repair output.

Pydantic alone cannot verify provenance. After schema validation, require all cited IDs to exist in the exact sent packet, require customer ID and recent-contact timestamp equality, and run deterministic prohibited-claim/business-truth checks before persistence.

### 4b.2 Async-First Design

`urllib.request.urlopen` is synchronous. Keep it off the AppKit/UI event loop by awaiting it in the existing background worker boundary; an asyncio host may use:

```python
import asyncio


async def analyze_async(client, model, messages):
    return await asyncio.to_thread(client.complete_json, model, messages, 1400)
```

The common mistake is calling `urlopen` directly on the UI/event-loop thread, which freezes progress and cancellation handling. Do not call `asyncio.run()` from an already running event loop; await the coroutine from its owner. Use `stream=False` and await the complete response because schema validation, `finish_reason`, and exact usage require the final envelope. UX progress should come from local request-state events, not token streaming.

### 4b.3 Prompt Engineering Discipline

Keep immutable behavioral rules and the JSON example in the versioned system prompt. Put canonical `BUSINESS_TRUTH` in a separate system message and the one candidate's canonical evidence packet in the user message. Never interpolate instructions from chat text into the system layer; evidence is data even when it contains prompt-like text.

Use one reviewed inline example because the schema is fixed. Do not retrieve dynamic few-shot examples: there is no RAG corpus for demonstrations, and retrieved examples could leak another customer's facts or contaminate evidence IDs. Set `max_tokens=1400` explicitly in production and version any change. The request changes `temperature` only; do not also tune `top_p`.

### 4b.4 Context Window Management

This hybrid extraction/generation system does not use RAG, conversation memory, agents, or framework compaction. Build one bounded packet from Phase 2 evidence, keep evidence records atomic, and reject an oversized packet before network I/O. Do not use model summarization as compaction because it would introduce uncited claims before the audited analysis. If future measured datasets justify different evidence selection, version that deterministic selector and reevaluate it rather than adding an invisible overflow path.

### 4b.5 Cost and Latency Budget

Use one provider call per analyzed candidate and no repair, routing, or summarization calls. Set a 120-second transport timeout; timeout is a surfaced failure, not permission to retry. Record queue time, network duration, validation duration, persistence duration, and total duration without recording prompt contents.

The runtime ledger always uses the user's configured price snapshot. As a July 23, 2026 reference only, DeepSeek lists `deepseek-v4-flash` at USD $0.0028/1M cache-hit input tokens, $0.14/1M cache-miss input tokens, and $0.28/1M output tokens. At an illustrative 2,000 cache-miss input tokens plus 300 output tokens, one call costs exactly $0.000364; 244 Phase 2 candidates would cost $0.088816. This is a planning estimate, never the recorded actual. Actual cost is:

```text
(cache_hit_tokens × configured_hit_price
 + cache_miss_tokens × configured_miss_price
 + completion_tokens × configured_output_price) / 1,000,000
```

Do not add a response cache or semantic cache. Each operator-started analysis run is an explicit new evaluation; request hashes provide audit identity and duplicate detection inside that run, not permission to reuse a stale judgment. A near match can change evidence IDs, customer identity, business truth, or draft claims.

Keep `deepseek-v4-flash` as the explicit default for this bounded task. Do not automatically route to `deepseek-v4-pro`, deprecated aliases, or another provider, and do not add extra cheap-model calls for classification, routing, or summarization. Any model change is a user-visible configuration change followed by evaluation, not a fallback.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

Evaluation answers one product question: can the system turn one bounded customer-evidence packet into a useful sales judgment without inventing facts, leaking extra chat content, accepting an invalid response, or corrupting the cost ledger? Model scores are evaluated as relative decision support, not conversion probabilities.

### 5.1 Release-gate dimensions

| Dimension | Priority | Domain rubric | Measurement | Release gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strict structure and task completion | Critical | **PASS:** exactly one `finish_reason="stop"` choice validates without coercion against `LeadJudgment`; score and band agree; actionable bands contain one evidenced draft; non-actionable bands contain none. **FAIL:** empty, partial, fenced, extra-field, wrong-type, wrong-band, or conditionally incomplete output is repaired, retried, persisted, or displayed as success. | Code: response-envelope fixtures plus strict Pydantic validation. | 100% of valid fixtures accepted and 100% of malformed/adversarial fixtures rejected; zero repair or retry calls. |
| Evidence-to-judgment traceability and factual grounding | Critical | **PASS:** customer ID and recent-contact timestamp are unchanged, every cited ID belongs to the exact packet, direction and recency support the score/action/draft, and conflicting or newer evidence remains reflected. **FAIL:** an unknown/other-customer ID is cited, store-authored language or negation becomes customer intent, a stale signal overrides a newer rejection, or an inference is stated as fact. | Code for identity/membership; calibrated LLM Judge for semantic support; Human review for conflicts. | Zero code-detectable mismatch; at least 95% judge/human groundedness pass rate; zero critical intent inversion. |
| Follow-up priority grounded in buying behavior | High | **PASS:** recent customer-authored price, trial, schedule, location, payment, availability, and contact signals outrank vague social chat; obstacles, rejection, incompatibility, and staleness lower priority. **FAIL:** a friendly non-buyer outranks a customer arranging a trial, or keywords alone promote outbound, negated, resolved, or stale evidence. | LLM Judge on explicit rubric, calibrated against store-owner labels; Human pairwise review. | At least 90% pairwise ordering agreement and at least 85% four-band agreement with domain experts; `成交意向分` is never labeled probability. |
| Truthful, proportionate activation draft and user-facing safety | Critical | **PASS:** only actionable candidates receive a natural editable draft using current `BUSINESS_TRUTH`, cited customer needs, one feasible next step, and no unnecessary private detail. **FAIL:** any price, offer, inventory, schedule, teacher, urgency, outcome, customer need, or commitment is invented; a rejecting customer is pressured; or private details make the message intrusive. | Code for forbidden claims, prices/offers, actionable-band rules, and cited needs; calibrated LLM Judge for intrusiveness/tone; Human compliance review. | Zero unsupported commercial/training claim; zero draft for non-actionable leads; at least 95% calibrated judge/human acceptance, with any critical compliance failure blocking release. |
| Minimum-necessary private-data handling | Critical | **PASS:** the request contains only versioned rules, canonical business truth, and whole locally selected records for one customer within configured evidence/character/byte limits; secrets and unrelated history remain local. **FAIL:** a full archive, another customer's evidence, unrelated sensitive facts, API key, or under-14 data without approved guardian/dedicated handling reaches transport. | Code: serialized-request inspection, allowlist and size assertions, secret canaries, transport-spy fixtures; Human privacy review of the packet contract. | 100% of outbound fixtures satisfy customer scope and limits; zero secret/cross-customer/unauthorized-child-data transmission. |
| Provider usage and exact cost ledger | Critical | **PASS:** provider cache-hit, cache-miss, prompt, completion, and total tokens reconcile; `Decimal` cost equals the stored price snapshot; one successful result, provenance record, and ledger entry commit atomically. Billable usage from a locally rejected provider response remains recorded against the failed run. **FAIL:** usage is reconstructed, float arithmetic changes totals, hidden second calls are omitted, or an unpersisted result appears successful. | Code: invariant fixtures, exact SQLite reconciliation, forced-rollback and duplicate-within-run tests. | Exact equality for every fixture and persisted call; at most one provider call per `(run_id, username)`; zero published result without one matching successful ledger entry. |

LLM judges are advisory until calibrated on the human-labeled reference set with agreement of at least 0.80 Cohen's kappa. Judge disagreement never overrides a deterministic safety failure; it routes the example to human review and the reference dataset.

### 5.2 Reference dataset

Create the dataset during Phase 3 implementation, before prompt tuning, and keep immutable packet/label versions under `evals/phase3/`. The initial production gate contains **32 cases**:

- 12 critical-path customer packets covering price, trial, schedule, location, payment, availability, contact exchange, explicit obstacle, and actionable/non-actionable bands.
- 8 domain edge cases covering store-vs-customer direction, negation, later rejection, stale interest, contradictory evidence, schedule incompatibility, weak social chat, and insufficient evidence.
- 6 privacy/safety/adversarial cases covering prompt injection inside evidence, unknown evidence IDs, unsupported price/offer/outcome claims, intrusive personalization, cross-customer contamination, and under-14 information without approved handling.
- 6 response/ledger cases covering empty or malformed JSON, non-`stop` completion, missing/inconsistent usage, exact cache-hit/miss pricing, duplicate request identity, and transaction rollback.

The store owner or senior sales operator labels band, pairwise priority, material evidence, obstacles, and next action. The enrollment lead labels draft naturalness and intrusiveness. Operations/finance labels token and `Decimal` cost truth. The privacy/advertising reviewer approves critical safety cases. Code-derived labels cover schema, identity, membership, usage, cost, and atomicity. Add every confirmed production failure to this set before changing prompts or thresholds; never rewrite expected labels merely to make a new model pass.

## 6. Guardrails

Guardrails are deliberately limited to failures that would make a displayed judgment, transmitted packet, or financial record unsafe. Each guardrail blocks the current operation with a stable error; none retries, repairs, truncates, changes model/provider, or publishes partial success.

### 6.1 Online guardrails — every request

| Guardrail | Runs | Deterministic check | Blocking action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Packet scope and privacy gate | Before network I/O | One customer only; evidence IDs belong to the published Phase 2 corpus/customer; whole-record count, per-excerpt character, and canonical-byte limits pass; outbound keys are allowlisted; no API-key/secret canary, cross-customer record, or unauthorized under-14 data is present. | Do not call DeepSeek; record a local stable error such as `AI_CONTEXT_TOO_LARGE`, `AI_PACKET_SCOPE_INVALID`, or `AI_SENSITIVE_DATA_BLOCKED`. |
| Single-call/provider contract gate | At request claim and transport | One `(run_id, username)` maps to at most one provider attempt; configured Base URL/model are sent unchanged; `stream=false`, thinking disabled, JSON mode and bounded `max_tokens` are present. | Reject a duplicate candidate inside the run or invalid configuration; never retry, route, or change the provider/model. |
| Envelope, schema, and provenance gate | Immediately after response | Exactly one choice, `finish_reason="stop"`, non-empty JSON, strict schema, customer/timestamp equality, unique cited IDs, and cited-ID membership all pass. | Persist terminal failure metadata only; do not expose a judgment or parse repaired content. |
| Commercial-truth and activation-safety gate | Before result persistence | Band/draft conditional rules pass; prices/offers/forbidden claims are checked against current business truth; attributed customer needs occur in cited evidence; deterministic prohibited training promises and unauthorized child-data use are absent. | Reject the result with the exact rule code; no draft or score becomes available for outreach. |
| Usage, cost, and atomic-publish gate | Before commit/publish | Provider token decomposition reconciles exactly; all prices parse as `Decimal`; cost recomputes exactly from the stored snapshot; judgment, provenance, call ledger, and run totals commit in one SQLite transaction. | Roll back the transaction and fail the analysis; actual cost remains null for calls without valid provider usage, and no unpersisted result is shown. |

Human preview remains mandatory before Phase 6 sending. This phase produces drafts only and has no path that sends them automatically.

### 6.2 Offline quality flywheel

| Signal | Batch evaluation | Owner and improvement action |
|---|---|---|
| Priority and band drift | Weekly pairwise agreement, four-band agreement, and false-high/false-low review on sampled locally stored results; segment by signal type, recency, and contradiction. | Store owner updates reference labels or proposes a versioned prompt/selector change; rerun the full gate before release. |
| Grounding and draft quality | Calibrated judge scores evidence support, action feasibility, natural WeChat tone, pressure, and intrusive personalization; humans inspect all judge disagreements and critical flags. | Enrollment lead and compliance reviewer add confirmed failures to the dataset; change business truth or a versioned rule, never post-process model prose into apparent compliance. |
| Schema/provider drift | Aggregate empty content, non-`stop`, schema, evidence, returned-model, and usage-validation failures by prompt/schema/model/base-origin version. | Engineering investigates the exact provider contract; no automatic repair or alternate model is introduced. |
| Privacy minimization | Weekly local audit of packet-builder field allowlist, evidence/byte distributions, and cross-customer/secret-canary tests without retaining transmitted chat bodies in telemetry. | Privacy owner tightens the deterministic selector/contract and records the approved change. |
| Cost and cache behavior | Recompute every ledger row from stored integer tokens and decimal price snapshot; trend per-call/run cost, cache-hit ratio, duplicate request hashes, and estimate-vs-actual. | Finance/engineering corrects configuration or transaction logic; historical immutable ledger rows are not rewritten. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

Production observability is local-first. JSONL structured events and the immutable SQLite call ledger are the complete authoritative records. Recorded operational fields are limited to request/run/call hashes or IDs, base URL origin, requested/returned model, prompt/schema/selector/config versions, status/error code, `finish_reason`, integer token fields, exact cost text, and timings. Customer evidence, raw responses, and secrets are prohibited from logs; customer-visible results remain only in the local product database.

### 7.1 Metrics and alert thresholds

| Metric | Threshold | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Invalid output accepted, unknown evidence accepted, customer/timestamp mismatch accepted, unsupported commercial/training claim accepted, or unauthorized packet transmitted | **Any occurrence** | Critical alert; block publication, preserve non-sensitive hashes/versions, and require human incident review before the affected prompt/model/config is used again. |
| Published result/ledger atomicity or exact cost reconciliation mismatch | **Any row** | Critical alert; stop publishing the run and reconcile the SQLite transaction path. Never estimate missing actual usage. |
| Provider attempts per `(run_id, username)` | **Greater than 1** | Critical alert for forbidden retry/duplicate call; stop the run and inspect run ownership. |
| API key, customer text, draft text, or raw response detected in logs/traces | **Any occurrence** | Critical privacy incident; stop tracing, remove the exposed local trace data, rotate the affected key when applicable, and review instrumentation before restart. |
| Empty/non-`stop`/schema/usage validation failures | Warning at **>5% over the latest 20 calls**; critical at **3 consecutive** or **>20% over 20 calls** | Inspect by returned model, prompt/schema version, base-origin, and error code; surface provider failure without retry. |
| Pairwise priority agreement / four-band expert agreement | Warning below **90% / 85%** on the rolling labeled set | Suspend prompt/model promotion; human-review disagreements and add confirmed cases to the frozen dataset. |
| Grounded, safe draft acceptance | Warning below **95%**; critical on any unsupported price, offer, inventory, commitment, or guaranteed outcome | Prevent affected prompt/model version from promotion; compliance review is required for the critical case. |
| Total latency | Warning when p95 exceeds **120 seconds** over 20 calls or any call exceeds the configured timeout without a terminal failure | Inspect queue/network/validation/persistence spans; do not stream, retry, or route around the timeout. |
| Actual cost and usage drift | Critical if actual cost does not exactly recompute, if run total differs from call sum, or if actual exceeds the same-call configured maximum estimate; warning if median cost rises **>25%** versus the prior 50 comparable calls | Verify price snapshot, token fields, model/config version, and cache-hit ratio. Do not alter historical rows. |

### 7.2 Sampling and review cadence

- Run deterministic online guardrails and ledger reconciliation on **100%** of requests and persisted calls.
- Record metadata-only spans for **100%** of calls, including terminal failures; record no customer content.
- Each week evaluate **20% of successful judgments or at least 20, whichever is larger** when volume permits, weighted to all high-intent results, all drafts containing configured prices/offers, unusually high/low scores, long packets, and contradictory/stale evidence.
- Human-review at least **10 sampled judgments per week**, plus every judge disagreement, every operator-rejected or materially edited draft once Phase 4 exposes that signal, and every critical alert.
- Recalibrate the LLM judge quarterly and after any prompt, schema, selector, model, or business-rule change. Require at least 0.80 Cohen's kappa against fresh human labels before its scores contribute to release decisions.
- Promote confirmed production failures into the versioned local reference dataset before implementing a correction, then rerun the complete deterministic gate. This is the evaluation flywheel and the only path for changing evaluated behavior.
